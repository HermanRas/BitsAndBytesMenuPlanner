T-Bone Steak, Potato Salad & Garlic Bread

Dinner Cooked

A weekend favourite — juicy grilled T-bone with a creamy homemade potato salad and buttery garlic bread on the side.

Ingredients

  • Steak (T-bone)1.5 kg · R180
  • Potatoes1 kg · R25
  • Mayonnaise1 jar · R45
  • Onion2 · R8
  • Eggs6 · R30
  • Garlic bread1 loaf · R35
  • Steak spice1 packet · R20
  • Garlic1 bulb · R10
  • Butter250 g · R35

Estimated cost: R388

Preparation

  1. Season the steaks with steak spice and let them rest at room temperature for 10 minutes.
  2. Boil the potatoes until just tender, then cool and dice.
  3. Mix the potatoes with mayonnaise, chopped egg and onion to make the potato salad.
  4. Grill or pan-fry the steaks to your preferred doneness.
  5. Butter the garlic bread with garlic and butter, then toast until golden.
  6. Serve the steak with the potato salad and garlic bread on the side.
